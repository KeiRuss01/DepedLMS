Help and Support

User Guide

This guide explains the main tasks available in the Hybrid Learning Management System. The features shown in an account depend on the user's assigned role.

Student

  1. Create an account, complete the required learner information, and sign in.
  2. Join a class using the class code provided by the Teacher.
  3. Open published modules and activities from the class page.
  4. Submit answers or attachments before the stated deadline.
  5. Check grades, Teacher feedback, attendance, announcements, and deadline notifications.

Parent or Guardian

  1. Create a Parent account and request to link the correct learner.
  2. Wait for the authorized school user to approve the link.
  3. View the linked learner's grades, attendance, feedback, and school announcements.

Teacher

  1. Sign in using the account created by the Principal.
  2. Create and manage classes, then approve or remove class members when necessary.
  3. Publish modules and activities, record attendance, review submissions, and provide feedback.
  4. Maintain the class gradebook and finalize the year-end grade report for Principal review.
  5. Archive a completed class to keep it available as a record without showing it as active.

Principal

  1. Update the school and personal profile after receiving an account from the District Supervisor.
  2. Create Teacher accounts and monitor the school's Teachers, Students, classes, and Parent links.
  3. Manage school announcements and calendar events.
  4. Review finalized year-end class reports and return reports that require correction.

District Supervisor

  1. Register schools and create Principal accounts.
  2. Publish district announcements and calendar events.
  3. View read-only, aggregate school monitoring and year-end reports.
Need assistance? Contact the assigned Teacher or school administrator. Never share your password with another person.

Terms of Service

By creating or using an account, the user agrees to use the Hybrid Learning Management System for authorized educational and school-administration purposes.

1. Account responsibility

Users must provide accurate information, protect their login credentials, and promptly report suspected unauthorized access. Accounts may not be shared or used to impersonate another person.

2. Acceptable use

Users must not upload harmful or unlawful material, harass other users, interfere with the system, attempt to bypass access controls, or access records that their role does not authorize them to view.

3. Educational records and content

Learning materials, submissions, attendance records, grades, comments, and reports must be used only for legitimate educational purposes. Users remain responsible for the content they submit.

4. Availability and synchronization

Some functions may depend on an internet connection. Offline changes, when supported, may remain pending until the device reconnects and completes synchronization.

5. Account restriction

An authorized school administrator may restrict or deactivate an account when required for security, inaccurate registration, misuse, withdrawal from the school, or violation of these terms.

6. Updates

These terms may be updated when the system, school procedures, or applicable requirements change. Material changes should be communicated through the system or the school.

Privacy Policy

The Hybrid Learning Management System processes school information to provide learning, grading, attendance, communication, and authorized school-monitoring functions.

1. Information collected

Depending on the user's role, the system may collect a name, email address, mobile number, school assignment, Learner Reference Number, birth date, address, relationship to a learner, class membership, attendance, submissions, grades, feedback, and account activity.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and verify accounts, deliver learning materials, manage classes, record attendance and grades, review submissions, link Parents to learners, send in-system notices, prepare authorized reports, protect accounts, and troubleshoot the service.

3. Role-based access

Information is shown only to users whose assigned role and school relationship require access. Students access their own records; approved Parents access linked learners; Teachers access their classes; Principals access their assigned school; and District Supervisors access authorized district-level information and aggregate reports.

4. Storage and protection

Reasonable technical and administrative safeguards are used, including authenticated accounts, password hashing, role-based permissions, and restricted record access. No system can guarantee absolute security, so users must also protect their devices and credentials.

5. Offline and device data

If offline access is enabled, limited learning data and pending actions may be stored on the user's device until synchronization. Users should sign out on shared devices and remove locally stored data when the device is no longer authorized.

6. Retention and correction

Records are retained only for as long as required for educational, administrative, research-testing, security, or legal purposes. Users may request correction of inaccurate personal information through the assigned school administrator.

7. Disclosure

Personal information is not sold. It may be disclosed only to authorized school or district personnel, service providers supporting the system under appropriate safeguards, or when disclosure is required by law or an authorized investigation.

8. Questions and requests

For access, correction, privacy, or account-deletion concerns, contact the user's school administrator or the North 1 District support contact shown on this website.

This policy is project-facing information and should be reviewed by the participating school and its designated data-protection personnel before production deployment.
